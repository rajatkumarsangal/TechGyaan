# Code of Conduct

TechGyaan is a learning-focused repository. Keep discussions respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people at different skill levels.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear and kind in reviews and discussions.
- Focus feedback on the content, code, or explanation.
- Assume learners may be new to the topic.
- Help improve accuracy, readability, and usefulness.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Spam or unrelated promotion.
- Publishing private information without permission.

Repository maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior does not support a healthy learning environment.
